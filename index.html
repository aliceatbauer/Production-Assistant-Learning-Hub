Before you log hours

Off-the-job learning should be new learning, relevant to your apprenticeship and completed within paid working hours. Record what you learned and how it develops your role, not simply the time spent. Agree suitability with your programme team or employer where needed.

KEEP YOUR EVIDENCE

Turn learning into evidence

After each activity, make a short note. A useful record answers four questions:

  1. What did I do? Describe the activity.
  2. What did I learn? Capture the new knowledge or skill.
  3. Why does it matter? Link it to your role or apprenticeship.
  4. What will I do differently? Record how you will apply it.
